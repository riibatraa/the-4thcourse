Why a table

You can't schedule your way into new friends. You have to sit down to a table where you don't already know the ending.

The first three courses are logistics — the ordering, the eating, the small talk. The fourth is where it turns: plates gone, the check ignored, the person across from you halfway through a story you'll retell for years. That course doesn't happen with your usual people. It happens with strangers chosen by how you actually show up at a table — not by a photo you'd post. That's the whole club.

No swiping

You get the seat or you don't. No matches, no inbox, no one to chase across an app.

No profiles

We never show a face, an age, or a bio. The night itself is the introduction.

No head

Round tables, always. Nobody hosts, nobody sits at the head — everyone is across from someone.

The format · two minutes

Three steps to your fourth course.

i

Take the seating quiz

Eight questions, three minutes. We read you across five ways of being at a table — not a profile, a place setting.

A fingerprint, not a label. It decides who you sit with, and who you don't.
ii

Every Thursday at 7:07 PM your WhatsApp buzzes. Inside: the venue, the night, your one-line persona. No faces yet.

One curated night per table per week. You don't pick the venue, the night, or the strangers.
iii

Show up. Stay for the fourth.

Turn up when the drop says. Pay your own bill. Stay as long as the table wants to. Tell us who you'd see again.

That last bit feeds the next match. The seating sharpens every week.

Honest answers

Why “the fourth course”?

Because the fourth course is the one you remember. The first three are the meal — the fourth is what happens after the plates are cleared, when no one leaves and the strangers have become friends. That's the part we're in the business of. The dinner is just how we get you there.

Is this a dating app?

No. Round tables of six, mixed, built for conversation. People meet partners sometimes — it isn't the point, and it isn't the promise.

How much does it cost?

₹1,499 a month, covering up to four weekend nights. You pay your own bill at the venue. Cancel anytime — no lock-in.

Why 7:07 on Thursday?

It's the drop. Same ritual every week so you know when to look — venue, night, and your one-line persona land on WhatsApp at exactly 7:07 PM.

Will someone from the 4thCourse be there?

No. The six at the table are all matched members. We do the matching, the venue, and the prompts behind the scenes. On the night, it's just the six of you.

What kinds of nights?

Suppers, wine bars, listening rooms, gallery openings, Sunday walks, the occasional late drive. One curated format per table per week.

What if my table doesn't click?

Leave whenever you like, and tell us after. Your next table will look nothing like this one — that's the design working, not failing.

How soon do I get my first seat?

The very next Thursday at 7:07, in most cases. No waitlist. If your cluster doesn't have six that week, it slides to the following Thursday.
